## Project Overview
A2Z Recyclers Website is a responsive and accessible environmental awareness platform developed for A2Z Recyclers, a sustainability-focused organisation promoting recycling habits and environmental responsibility.

The website aims to educate users about recycling benefits, showcase organisational initiatives, promote upcoming environmental events, and provide a user-friendly platform for community engagement.

---

## Objectives
- Promote recycling awareness and sustainability
- Educate users about environmental responsibility
- Encourage recycling habits in households and businesses
- Provide information about workshops, projects, and events
- Create an accessible and responsive user experience

---

## Features

### Home Page
- Modern responsive landing page
- Mission statement and vision section
- Interactive navigation menu
- Smooth scrolling user experience

### Recycling Benefits Section
Highlights the five major benefits of recycling:
- Reduces landfill waste
- Prevents pollution
- Conserves natural resources
- Saves energy
- Creates economic opportunities

### Values Section
Displays company core values:
- Commitment
- Teamwork
- Responsibility

### News & Events
- Upcoming recycling workshop information
- Event registration details
- Community engagement promotion

### Testimonials
Customer feedback and environmental partnership testimonials.

### Projects Undertaken
Showcases collaboration with Ecogenie and sustainable recycling initiatives.

### Contact Form
Interactive contact form with:
- Form validation
- Required field checks
- User-friendly error messaging
