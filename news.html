Workshop on Recycling

Don’t Miss These Green Changes

A workshop to teach kids basic recycling habits is coming up on Friday 16th November to Sunday 18th November at the Baden Powell Scout Centre, Pennant Hills, NSW.

This is our major event of the year. The minister of environment of Australia will be present at the event along with some members of the UNESCO. The age group for participation would be 7 to 18 years.

Kids below the age of 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
